## 1-4 Jerusalem Mother


### Chapter 1

This time, let's study about "Jerusalem Mother". I would like to share the word of God with you together.

God has given us a priceless promise. So the Bible calls us the children of promise.

Let's take a look at Gal chapter 4 verse 28. It says...

_**Gal 4:28 Now you, brothers and sisters, like Isaac, are children of promise.**_

We are children of promise since we've received a promise from God. What is the promise of God?

Let's take a look at 1Jn chapter 2 verse 25. It says...

_**1Jn 2:25 And this is what he promised us - eternal life.**_

What is God's promise? It is eternal life. Then, let's find out how we can receive eternal life.


### Chapter 2

Let's begin by looking at Rev chapter 4 verse 11. It says...

_**Rev 4:11 You're worthy, our Lord and God, to receive glory and honor and power, for you created all things, and by your will they were created and have their being.**_

By whose will were all things created? They were created by God's will. Among the things God created, let's think about life.

Countless living things on earth receive life from their fathers and mothers. There is not a single creature that receives life only from a father without a mother.

Birds in the sky, fish in the sea, animals on the ground. They all have a mother.

The same is true for human beings. Is there anyone in this world born without a mother? None. Absolutely not.

Yes, there must be a father, but ultimately it is the mother that gives birth to her child. Mothers play a key role in the deliverance of life.

God said that He created all things by His will. Then what is God's will in creating mothers?

It was to show us through whome we can receive the promise of eternal life.

Just as our physical life is given by our physical mothers, our spiritual life is given by our spiritual Mother.

Thus, for us, who are children of promise, ther must exist our spiritual Mother who will give us eternal life as God promised.


### Chapter 3

Up to now, we have studied the will of God through the creation and can see that our spiritual Mother exists.

From now on, let's confirm the existense of our spiritual Mother through the Bible.

Let's take a look at Ge chapter 1 verse 26. It says...

_**Ge 1:26 Then God said, "Let us make mankind in our image, in our likeness."**_

God said, "Let us make mankind in our image, in our likeness." God did not refer to Himself as "I" in a singular form, but rather as "Us" in a plural form.

Then, why did God refer to Himself in a plural form, not a singular form? For this, let's see verse 27. It says...

_**Ge 1:27 So God created mankind in his own image, in the image of God he created them; male and female he created them.**_

In verse 26, God said, "Let us make mankind in our image." and in verse 27, mankind who was created in the image of God was male and female.

The fact that males and females were created in the image of God means that God has two images: a male image and a female image, doesn't it?

For ages, believers have thought that God only exists as a male, so they have called God "Father."

However, the Bible testifies not only about the male image of God, but also about the female image of God.

This is why the Bible refers to God as "Us."

Then, What should we call the female image of God?

Of course, we should call Her "Mother."

According to the Bible, we must believe in our spiritual Mother as well as our spiritual Father.


### Chapter 4

We've learned that all things were created by God's will. Then, What is God's will in creating Adam and Eve who were made in the image of God?

First, let's find out what God's will was in creating Adam.

Let's take a look at Ro chapter 5 verse 14. It says...

_**Ro 5:14 Nevertheless, death reigned from the time of Adam to the time of Moses, even over those who did not sin by breaking a command, as did Adam, who is a pattern of the one to come.**_

Who does Adam, the first man created in the image of God, represent? He is a pattern of Jesus Christ who was to come to this earth.

Jesus is God the Father according to the Trinity. Then, Who does Eve, who was created in the image of God, represent?

As Adam represents God the Father, his wife, Eve, represents God the Mother. Let's find out what God's will is in creating Eve.

Let's take a look at Ge chapter 3 verse 20. It says...

_**Ge 3:20 Adam named his wife Eve, because she would become the mother of all the living.**_

What does the name "Eve" mean in Hebrew? It means living or life. (Refer to the footnote.)

Why does the Bible call the first woman "Eve" which means life?

In this world, there is one thing that a man can never do, but only woman can. It is to give birth to a child.

The crucial role of giving life to a child is given to a woman.

This is why the Bible calls the first woman Eve which means life and why it calls her the "mother of all the living."

As Adam is a prophetic figure of Jesus who is the male image of God, Eve is the prophetic figure of Heavenly Mother who is the female image of God.
